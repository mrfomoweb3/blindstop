# Blindstop demo film: three storylines

Common to all three: 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 3:00 (2:45–3:15), narrated by a HeyGen voice over a quiet music bed, sound on clicks. Every product shot is the real Blindstop app captured from the running build; any illustration is labelled. Look: the app's own (near-black ground, lime accent rationed to the seal and the payoff, Geist type).

Honesty constraint: no hidden stop has yet closed a funded Hyperliquid position. Until test USDC arrives, the "it fires" beat shows the real enclave report settled on NEAR (order 6, a real stop the enclave fired on testnet: "nothing to close") and the real-wasm harness run, labelled "simulation". If funds land before the render, that beat is replaced with the live fire.

---

## A · The note on the door (recommended: a stranger gets it in one image)

**Device:** an everyday analogy that becomes the product. **Form:** object-to-object chain: each shot ends on the thing that opens the next.

A trader's stop-loss is a handwritten note taped to a front door: "I'll sell everything at $94,800." Passers-by read it; a crowd gathers; the price is shoved into the note. Then the note folds into a sealed envelope, the envelope drops into Blindstop, and the film walks through the real app: open a trade, hide the stop, Hyperliquid's public list showing nothing, the Status page live, a stop checked as genuine.

- **First 2 s:** a front door, a note taped to it in marker: "STOP-LOSS: SELL AT $66,000". A second shadow falls across it.
- **Beats:** note on the door (hook) → the crowd and the shove: stop hunting (tension) → the note folds into an envelope: "Blindstop" (reveal) → real app: sign in, trading wallet, open a trade, hide the stop, public list = 0 (proof) → the sealed computer watching, the stop firing, the signed receipt on NEAR (payoff) → check a stop: Genuine → claim → end card.
- **Look:** a warm, paper-and-door world for the analogy, cutting through the envelope into the app's dark lime world, which then holds for the rest.
- **Sound:** calm narrator, soft pulse bed; a paper rustle, a tape rip, a seal "thunk", soft UI clicks.

## B · The hunt, live on the chart

**Device:** a number lands, and the chart is the explanation. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds: a single price chart never leaves the screen.

One BTC price chart fills the frame. Dashed lines appear: other traders' stop-losses, bunching into a cluster. The price is driven down into the cluster, triggering every line, then bounces. Rewind. The same chart, but the stop is hidden: there is nothing to aim at. The chart shrinks into the Blindstop app's position card, and the walkthrough happens around it.

- **First 2 s:** a live-looking BTC chart with a thick band of red dashed lines labelled "public stop-losses".
- **Beats:** the cluster forms → the shove and the bounce → rewind, the line vanishes: "Blindstop" → the chart becomes the app: trade, hide, public list = 0 → the price reaches the hidden level: closed, receipt on NEAR → proof: Genuine → claim → end card.
- **Look:** one chart, the app's dark ground, red for public stops, lime for the hidden one.
- **Sound:** a ticking bed that tightens as the price falls, a hard hit at the bounce, then calm.

## C · The journey of one stop (the bold one)

**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** a flight through one world: the camera follows one stop-loss from the moment it is typed to its receipt.

The camera rides along with a single stop price. It is typed into the app, locked into an envelope in the browser, sent to NEAR as 316 bytes of ciphertext, picked up by the sealed computer, held up against Hyperliquid's live price every three seconds, and finally fired, coming back as a signed receipt NEAR verifies. Each stop on the route is a real screen or the real artefact.

- **First 2 s:** extreme close-up of the number "94,800" being typed into the stop-loss field, the cursor blinking.
- **Beats:** typed → locked → stored on NEAR (ciphertext visible, price not) → inside the sealed computer → watching the price → fire → receipt verified → claim → end card.
- **Look:** a continuous dark space with the lime path drawn as the route; each stop is a real UI panel at 1.6–2× size.
- **Sound:** a travelling bed with a soft whoosh on each hand-off, a lock click, a seal thunk at the receipt.
